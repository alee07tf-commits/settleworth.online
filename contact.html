Contact us

Questions about a settlement calculator on this site, about the method behind a figure, a correction to our data, or a partnership? We would like to hear from you. We answer method questions; we do not take cases and we give no legal advice.

How to reach us

Email us at alee07tf@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. We welcome feedback on our calculators, questions about how a figure is produced, corrections to our data or methodology, and press or partnership enquiries.

We usually reply within a few business days. To help us help you, mention which calculator you used and your question or correction. Please do not send sensitive personal or case details by email.

What we can & can't help with

SettleWorth is a neutral, informational tool — not a law firm. We can explain how our calculators work and the methods behind them, but we cannot give legal advice, review the facts of your case, tell you what your specific claim is worth, or refer you to an attorney. For advice about your situation, please consult a licensed attorney in your state. See our methodology, Terms of use and Privacy policy.
