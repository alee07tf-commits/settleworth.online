Privacy policy

SettleWorth is built to need as little of your data as possible. No account, no sign-up, and we never sell your information.

What we collect

Our calculators run entirely in your browser. When you enter figures such as medical bills or wages, those values are processed on your device to show your estimate and are not transmitted to us, stored, or sold. We do not ask for, and do not require, your name, email or phone number to use any tool on this site.

Like most websites, our servers may automatically record basic, non-identifying technical information (such as browser type, device type and pages visited) for security and to understand aggregate traffic.

Cookies & analytics

We may use privacy-respecting analytics to measure aggregate, anonymised usage (for example, which calculators are most used). These tools may set cookies or similar identifiers. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time without losing access to the calculators.

Advertising & third parties

SettleWorth may display advertising, including via Google AdSense, to keep the site free. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn more about third-party cookies at aboutads.info. We do not control the cookies set by these third parties.

Your choices & contact

You can use SettleWorth without providing any personal information. To ask a question about this policy or request information, contact us at [email pendiente] (see our contact page). We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
