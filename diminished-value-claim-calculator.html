What this estimates. Diminished value is the gap between what your car was worth before the crash and its lower market value after repair — money you can often claim from the at-fault driver's insurer. This is a property-damage tool, not an injury tool: it values your car, not your injuries. See also: For injury losses from the same crash, use the car accident settlement calculator or the truck accident settlement calculator.

How the 17c diminished value formula works

Start from 10% of your car's value, then apply a damage multiplier and a mileage multiplier.
